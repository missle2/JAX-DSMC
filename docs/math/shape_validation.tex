\begin{frame}{How to read this deck}
\small
\textbf{Arbiters}, from strongest to weakest:
\begin{itemize}
\item analytic or exact references (closed forms, Rankine--Hugoniot, exact quadrature, replica-exact
  value gradients on collisionless cases);
\item \textbf{CRN FD}: central finite difference on the \emph{same} seeds (common random numbers),
  paired with the estimator seed by seed; noise grows like $1/h$ across hit-set jumps;
\item \textbf{FD-only}: finite differences over many independent seeds (no pairing).
\end{itemize}
$z$ = (estimator $-$ arbiter) / SE of the paired difference.  ``Seeds'' always means independent
forward runs.  Lesson learned repeatedly: never conclude from 2 seeds.

\section{II. Parameter gradients}

\begin{frame}{The trajectory (DiCE) adjoint, M6--M8 (2026-09-05 \dots 09-11)}
\footnotesize
Score-function plus pathwise AD through the whole run (\code{jax\_dsmc/adjoint.py}).
\begin{itemize}
\item Oracles: [P1] Algorithm 2 and [P2] Algorithm 3 against \code{jax.grad} exactly
  ($10^{-11}$, $10^{-10}$); move$\to$collide recursion to $10^{-16}$ (Oracle 3).
\item Pathwise wall gradients: $\dd J/\dd T_{\mathrm{wall}}$, $\dd J/\dd u_{\mathrm{wall}}$ to
  $3\times10^{-12}$ against fixed-key FD, and against the Couette closed form (figure).
\item Inflow \code{nrho} (score): FD/AD $= 1.04$, $|z| = 0.81$.
\item \textbf{Attribution bias}: pairing each score with its own particle's reward is exact without
  collisions ($|z| = 0.18$) and $2.8\times$ low with them ($|z| = 22$), growing with the horizon
  (FD/AD $1.13 \to 2.82$ from 2 to 40 steps).  Accommodation biased $2.8\times$; shock
  $\dd(\rho_2/\rho_1)/\dd u_1$ \textbf{wrong sign}.
\item VSS $\alpha$: argon's $\alpha = 1.40$ sits at the stationary point $\sqrt2$ of
  $\langle\cos^2\chi\rangle$; $\dd T_y/\dd\alpha\approx0$ is the right answer (AD tracks FD,
  $|z|\le0.96$, across $\alpha$).
\end{itemize}

\begin{frame}{Walls as kernels, kernel parameters, and the exact sequential pull-back}
\footnotesize

\textbf{Kernel parameters} (\code{diam}, \code{omega}): $\partial_\theta\log q$ on the accepted

\section{III. Shape gradient}

\begin{frame}{First shape oracles (2026-09-17/18)}
\footnotesize
\textbf{Planar oracle} (one step, collisionless, specular wall): analytic $\dd J/\dd\mathrm{gap} = -0.5917$.
The pathwise gradient at frozen hits reads \textbf{0} (the hit set is invisible to it); the
value-weighted $\varepsilon$-hit score $0.588\pm0.008$.

\textbf{G3a window score} (1-D closed box, windowed impulse): the $\varepsilon$ hit-timing score has no
resolvable mean on a steady window and costs 4--8$\times$ the FD's sd $\Rightarrow$ the shape runs use
$\varepsilon = 0$; the hit set enters through the replayed wall map and band terms instead.

\medskip

\begin{frame}{G3: a translated wall in a 2-D Couette, and what it taught}
\footnotesize
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Hits change step.}  Windowed $v_y^2$: pathwise alone $+0.82\pm0.23$ vs FD
  $+0.05\pm0.15$; the hit-timing band term ($-0.43\pm0.02$) closes it.
\item \textbf{Two-sided fits are biased next to a wall.}  Windowed shear came out with the wrong sign
  ($-0.012$ vs $+0.017$); nodes restricted to the leaving side ($v\cdot n > 0$) fix the sign.
\item \textbf{Bias or horizon?}  With replica-exact value gradients the estimator equals the
  \emph{truncated} FD on both objectives ($z = 0.5$); the gap to the full FD is the captured
  horizon (72 captured steps before the window close it).
\item \textbf{Estimators of $\nabla_x\lambda$} against replica-exact truth (windowed $v_y^2$, exact
  $x/v$ part $+0.374\pm0.111$): one-sided $-0.29\pm0.17$ bias, pooled $-0.20\pm0.11$, one-sided +
  along-flight $-0.08\pm0.11$, pooled + along-flight $-0.07\pm0.11$.  The along-flight identity holds
  per particle ($-1.3\mathrm{e}{-5}\pm2.8\mathrm{e}{-5}$ on a scale $1.6\mathrm{e}{-3}$).
\item \textbf{Unresolved}: the $x/v$ part of a \emph{translated flat wall} (a window-end delay
  sensitivity); every fitted or value-based form reads 0.
\end{enumerate}
\textbf{Decisions (2026-09-20)}: shape controls are normal displacements of curved surfaces; the
body is a $C^1$ level set (a polygon's vertex term is a representation artefact).
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{The specular capsule and the future-class fit (2026-09-22, superseded)}
\footnotesize
\textbf{Specular capsule}: mixed-pool fits read $-27\,\%$ ($z = -11$); the tally alone matched the FD
seed by seed, and the deficit sat in the $\nabla\lambda$ parts.  Cause: at the curved cap the one-sided
rule does not separate leaving particles from those heading for the flat face.

\textbf{Stratifying the fits by ``a body hit at a later step''} fixed it:
$0.964\pm0.037 \to 1.356\pm0.011$ vs FD $1.35$ ($z = +0.08$), and became the default.

\medskip
\textbf{It was biased by construction} (found 2026-09-25, explained 2026-10-02): the class is a
property of the particle's future, so the fit drops $\nabla P\,(\lambda_1 - \lambda_0)$ (Part IV,
Finding 1).  On the capsule $P\approx0$ after a reflection, so it was harmless there; on the fine
$\cos2\theta$ mode (32 seeds, cluster) it read $-2.386\pm0.005$ against FD $-1.592$
($z\approx-120$), the tally alone matching the FD ($z = -0.33$).

\medskip
\textbf{Consequences}: the \code{optc} optimisations ran with the class; the squash studies did not.
The class is now a diagnostic option that warns.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Finding 7: the order of a hit and the step's collisions}
\small
NTC collides once per step, after the move.  A hit that slips from step $k$ into $k+1$ collides in
step $k$ as the incoming particle instead of the reflected one:
$J_c = c(x_w,\vin) - c(x_w,\vout)$, $c = \Delta t\,\Lstar\lambda$, at every step boundary.  The
window-edge reward jump is the collisionless remainder of the same event.

\medskip
Kn 0.5, $s = 1$, 8 seeds: $+6.3\pm4.4\times10^{-5}$ $= +0.4\pm0.3\,\%$ of $\dd D/\dd s$.  Small,
because the incoming and reflected collision effects differ by a small fraction of the reward; in
the gradient by default.

\medskip

get no reward in $\lambda$ and no VJP.  Their share is recorded; zero while the body is more than
$|v|\Delta t$ from the inflow and outflow faces; nonzero only for the \code{optc} Adam bodies that
reached those faces.  Now warns above $10^{-3}$.
\end{frame}

\section{V. The field interpolant of the collision pull-back}

\begin{frame}{Why the interpolant was reopened (2026-10-09/10)}
\small
The virtual pull-back asks $(3+2K)N$ field values per substep ($19N$ at $4\times8$): the node's own value,
the two post-collision ghosts of each virtual partner, the boundary move.  Until 2026-10-11 each was a
\textbf{per-query} weighted quadratic fit over the $k$ nearest nodes of the query's cell.

\medskip
\textbf{Its answer depended on $k$} (Kn 0.5 squash, $s = 1$, $f_{\mathrm{num}}\times4$, paired, \% of $\dd D/\dd s$):

Over 12 seeds: $k = 32$ $-3.5\pm1.8\,\%$ vs FD, $k = 64$ $-2.0\pm2.0\,\%$ ($k$ 32$\to$64: $+1.6\pm0.7\,\%$,
