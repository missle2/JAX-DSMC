\begin{frame}{Data flow of one gradient evaluation (\code{ensemble\_mesh.shape\_terms})}
\small
\begin{enumerate}
\item \code{forward(cfg, ..., capture=True)}: warm-up, then \code{steps} captured steps
  $\to$ \code{EventRecord} (\code{M} steps $\times$ \code{N} slots, lean record).
\item \code{shape.replay}: re-runs $\mathcal W_\theta$ per captured step; per-hit rewards
  (\code{\_drag\_reward}), timing rewards (\code{\_drag\_jump}), replay error.
\item \code{\_backwards}: one \code{backward\_virtual} sweep with two value columns
  (hit rewards, timing rewards), \code{collision\_out=True}, \code{columns\_out=True}
  $\to$ \code{lam}, \code{lam\_timing} (f64[M, N]), \code{collision} (f32[M, N]),
  \code{cell\_collision} (f64[M, C]).
\item \code{shape.gradient\_variants(lam\_timing, collision=...)}: one scan over the
  captured steps, one VJP per step; all \code{VARIANTS} share the replay and the
  non-fitted cotangents $\to$ \code{ShapeTerms} per variant (incl.\ \code{order}).
\item \code{\_volume\_term}: \code{volume\_weights} + \code{volume\_cotangent}.
\item \code{control\_gradient}: the VJP of \code{levelset\_coef} for a scalar control;
  the optimiser of free shapes keeps the coefficient gradient.
\end{enumerate}
Memory on the 4\,GB workstation GPU: \code{fnum\_scale} $\times2$ with 300 captured steps is
the largest collisional squash case; production runs use the cluster.
\end{frame}
